🧠 Your Resources
Here are practical reminders designed to support our day-to-day work and help us improve consistency.
⏱️ Tempo — Weekly Discipline
Our goal is to revert and become the team with the fewest Empower discrepancies. To get there, we need consistent time tracking and strong evidence-sharing.
  • Every Friday (end of day): register your hours in Tempo before you log off.
  • Send your evidence/proofs (screenshots, links, or notes) to your leaders to support validations and reduce discrepancies.
  • Log time only on active Jira issues within your assigned project.
  • Agile ceremonies should be logged under Agile Ceremonies / Meetings EPIC.
  • Do not log PTO, holidays, or Globant activities.
